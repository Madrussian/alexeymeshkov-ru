Side: AI analysis of legal documents

A person uploads a court document or a claim; Side analyses it and drafts a response. In production since 15 June 2026. Built solo, from discovery and architecture to evals and distribution.

The pipeline runs from cheap steps to expensive ones, so each next step gets less work:

  1. OCR and parsing. PDFs and scans into structured text.
  2. Pseudonymisation. Four stages, personal data recall of 95%+. No name leaves the system — a requirement of Russia’s personal data law (152‑FZ).
  3. Classification on Claude Haiku. Identify the type of situation and filter out what the service must not help with.
  4. Analysis on Claude Sonnet. Assessment of the position and a draft document.
  5. Verification. An LLM judge and a stop classifier with target error rates: at most 1% misses, at most 15% false alarms.
70 test caseseval pipeline with an adversarial generator and a red‑team gate before release

Lab: when does evolution pay for a brain

Why nature needs a brain, and what that can teach AI. An essay on intelligence as a response to the environment, with the brain as only one way to respond. Perhaps AI needs not a brain, but a world in which a mind becomes necessary.

Prototype: a 2D world where a neuron costs energy. Brainless plants, herbivores and predators with tiny neural networks. I tested whether evolution grows bigger brains when the climate changes faster. Short answer: no, and it is clear why.
